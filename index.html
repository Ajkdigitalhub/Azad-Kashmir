Ratti Gali Lake

An alpine glacial lake fed by melting snow peaks, surrounded by vibrant wildflowers and lush green plateaus. Reachable via 4x4 jeep from Dowarian followed by a scenic 1-hour trek.

Best Season: July - Sept Verified Route
Poonch • 1,981m

Banjosa Lake

An artificial scenic lake set amidst dense pine forests and mountains, ideal for boating and family picnics. Located 18 km from Rawalakot city with fully paved roads.

Best Season: Year-Round Paved Access
