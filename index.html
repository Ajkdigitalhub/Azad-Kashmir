  • Altitude: lakes such as Ratti Gali are above 3,000 m. Climb gradually, drink water, and descend if headache or nausea gets worse.
  • Weather: mountain weather changes fast. Avoid river crossings after rain.
  • Roads: landslides can block valley roads in the monsoon. Start early and carry extra food and water.
  • Signal: mobile coverage can be weak. Tell someone your route and save 1122.
  • Respect locals: dress modestly, take your litter back, and ask before photographing people.

History of Azad Jammu & Kashmir

A factual summary of the events that shaped the region, with a list of primary documents and books. Read the PDF online or download it to keep.

Key dates

  • 1846Treaties of Lahore (9 March) and Amritsar (16 March): Kashmir passes from the Sikh state to the British and then to Gulab Singh, founder of the Dogra dynasty.
  • Spring 1947Uprising in Poonch against Dogra rule, beginning with a no-tax campaign.
  • 12 Aug 1947Maharaja Hari Singh proposes standstill agreements to India and Pakistan.
  • 3-4 Oct 1947A first provisional Azad Kashmir government is proclaimed; accounts differ on place and details.
  • 22 Oct 1947Tribal forces enter the state from the west and fighting spreads.
  • 24 Oct 1947A provisional government led by Sardar Muhammad Ibrahim Khan is established, commonly placed at Palandri.
  • 26 Oct 1947The Maharaja signs the Instrument of Accession to India, a step Pakistan disputes.
  • 21 Apr 1948UN Security Council Resolution 47 on the India-Pakistan question.
  • 1 Jan 1949Ceasefire takes effect.
  • 28 Apr 1949Karachi Agreement between Pakistan, the AJK government and the Muslim Conference sets administrative arrangements.
  • 27 Jul 1949Karachi Agreement of military representatives fixes the ceasefire line under UN supervision.
  • 1967Mangla Dam completed near Mirpur.
  • 2 Jul 1972Simla Agreement: the ceasefire line is redefined as the Line of Control.
  • 1974Azad Jammu and Kashmir Interim Constitution Act adopted by the AJK Legislative Assembly.
  • 8 Oct 2005A major earthquake devastates Muzaffarabad and nearby districts; large-scale reconstruction follows.
