Critical Tourist Preparedness Rules

warning
Monsoon & Winter Landslide Telemetry

During seasonal precipitation or sudden freezes, check SDMA Control (05822-921643) before departing Muzaffarabad for Neelum or Leepa Valley. Carry heavy-duty tow straps.

sim_card
SCO (SCOM) Cellular SIM Connectivity

National mainstream carriers possess limited or no coverage past Athmuqam in Upper Neelum. Procure an SCO (SCOM) SIM card in Muzaffarabad or Kohala bridge checkpoint for seamless 4G connectivity.

badge
Border Proximity & Foreign National Permits

Pakistani citizens require original computerized CNIC for all internal military and police checkposts. International passport holders must obtain NOC clearance via the Ministry of Interior/AJK Tourism Police.

tire_repair
Winter Driving & 4x4 Mountain Gearing

Low-clearance sedans must not attempt routes beyond Keran during November through April. Steel tire chains and 4-wheel low range transmission are strictly verified at police checkpoints.
